## 8. Critical protocol rules to preserve

Read the complete `task-5-rulings.md` before changing these boundaries. The brief below is navigation, not a replacement for exact wire contracts.

- Pairing admission C and independently accepted authorizing endpoint D are distinct. A provider header, roster or higher epoch does not supply authority. Recovery admission R and its accepted lineage must be validated exactly.
- **Current material activation is independent of history installation.** Preserve the accepted explicit `activate_current_membership_material` guards. The narrower rule is that an admission receipt alone must not automatically activate writes or assert history is installed. Do not reintroduce a blanket history-before-writes requirement.
- Recovery claims use canonical V2 CBOR, exact domain separation, the signed parent and original-session possession proof. No implicit V1 promotion, synthetic pairing confirmation, new crypto protocol or re-pairing of identity.
- All available historical keys may be sealed before phrase disposal. Never persist the phrase or root private key. Epoch-one provenance comes from the exact original root-authenticated pinned enrollment envelope, not an invented public genesis digest. Later epochs also require the exact public rotation commitment.
- Replacement of an incomplete recovery target is explicit, never automatic from provider downloads. It must preserve **every** earlier authenticated reconstruction/installation baseline. A verified A → incomplete B → regressive C sequence must reject C rather than forget A.
- Inconsistent receipt fields fail closed; missing or tampered fields do not prove that no earlier baseline existed.
- Count and aggregate storage limits include all retained candidates and final receipt signatures before transaction commit. Existing budgets include 4,096 events/pages, 64MiB categories, 100,000 operations, one million dependencies and a 1MiB checkpoint limit; consult the typed budgets for exact application.
- Historical checkpoint author authorization is evaluated on the verified historical lineage; do not silently require a currently active exporter.
- Server public initialization is atomic with committed enrollment and private. SQL canonical-offset extraction is tightly version/layout gated and independently tested; it does not replace mandatory Edge cryptographic verification. No provider-roster backfill or old-receipt reactivation.
